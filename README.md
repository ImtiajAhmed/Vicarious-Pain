Calibrate the Display
1. From the Oculus Home menu, press the Oculus button on the remote to open the Universal Menu. On the right side, select Change Lens Spacing by pressing the large circle (select) button on the Oculus remote.
2. When you see a green cross, adjust the side straps as needed until the horizontal lines of the cross look crisp and clear.
3. Locate the small slider on the bottom right of the front of the headset. Using your thumb, press and slide it left or right until the vertical lines of the green cross are crisp. This adjusts the spacing of the lenses to fit your eyes. When the cross looks as sharp as possible, press the select button on the remote to finish.


Shortcuts

R → Recenter the Oculus orientation
E → Clear the debug text
X → Quit the application
N → Skip to the next trial
